Privacy Policy – RETAILX 2.0

Effective date: 10 November 2026

1. Introduction

RETAILX 2.0 is retail billing and business management software provided by Retailx Business Solutions. It helps businesses manage sales, invoices, customers and inventory. We respect privacy and take reasonable steps to protect personal information processed through the software.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how a business uses RETAILX 2.0, the software may process customer names, mobile phone numbers, billing and invoice details, product and purchase information, transaction amounts, payment records, and business and inventory records.

3. WhatsApp Integration

RETAILX 2.0 can integrate with the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. A business using this feature may send invoice copies or relevant billing information to a customer through WhatsApp, including when the customer requests a bill. The business may also configure a designated WhatsApp number to receive bill copies.

When a WhatsApp message is sent, the relevant phone number, message content and invoice information are transmitted to Meta/WhatsApp to provide the messaging service. Users of RETAILX 2.0 should ensure they have an appropriate legal basis and any required permissions before sending customer information.

4. How Information Is Used

  • Creating and managing invoices and sales records.
  • Maintaining customer records and supporting business operations.
  • Managing products and inventory.
  • Sending invoices and billing messages through WhatsApp when the business chooses to use that feature.
  • Providing technical support, maintaining and securing the software.

5. Sharing of Information

Retailx Business Solutions does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the software or provide features requested by the business, including Meta/WhatsApp for WhatsApp message delivery, or where disclosure is required by law.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Business records may be retained for as long as needed for the business's operational, accounting and legal requirements. A customer who wants to access, correct or request deletion of their information should first contact the business that collected the information. For questions about RETAILX 2.0 or this policy, contact Retailx Business Solutions using the details below. Requests will be reviewed subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

8. Third-Party Services

WhatsApp features are subject to Meta's applicable terms and policies. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

RETAILX 2.0 is intended for business and retail operations and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published at the same public policy address, with the effective date updated when changes are made.
